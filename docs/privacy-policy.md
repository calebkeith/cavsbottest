# CavsBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [2026-09-27]

CavsBot ("CavsBot," "we," "us," or "our") is a fan-made Reddit application for viewing Cleveland Cavaliers schedules, scores, game information, standings, player information, and related basketball content. This Privacy Policy explains what information CavsBot handles when you use the app on Reddit.

## Information we handle

### Reddit account and context information

When the app is opened, CavsBot may receive information that Reddit makes available to the app for the current interaction, including your Reddit username, subreddit name, post ID, and post context. CavsBot uses this information to identify the Reddit context in which the app is running and to display a greeting or associate app activity with the relevant post.

CavsBot does not ask for your email address, password, payment information, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive personal information.

### App and operational data

CavsBot stores limited operational data in Reddit's Devvit storage service, including shared counters and cached basketball and game-thread data. Cache entries may include game IDs, game state, scores, play-by-play information, and other basketball data. The app does not use this storage to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

### Basketball data from external services

To provide basketball features, CavsBot sends requests to the CavsBot API hosted on Microsoft Azure. Requests may include basketball query parameters such as team, player, game, season, or date. CavsBot is configured to request basketball data from that service and does not intentionally send your Reddit username or other personal profile information to it.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- operate and display CavsBot features;
- return the requested basketball data;
- maintain caches and app-generated Reddit content;
- troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and maintain reliability; and
- comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

## Sharing and service providers

CavsBot does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising. Information may be processed by:

- Reddit, as the platform hosting the app and providing Reddit account and context information;
- Reddit Devvit storage and infrastructure, to operate the app; and
- Microsoft Azure, which hosts the CavsBot API used to retrieve basketball data.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. CavsBot may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce these terms, protect users or the service, or investigate abuse.

## Retention

Operational counters, cached data, and app-generated content are retained only as long as reasonably needed for the app's operation, subject to Reddit's infrastructure and retention practices. Some content may remain on Reddit after it is created or after the app is no longer available. We do not promise a specific deletion period for data stored or controlled by Reddit or Microsoft Azure.

## Your choices and requests

You may stop using CavsBot at any time. For questions or requests about information handled by CavsBot, contact **caleb.keith@outlook.com**. Requests concerning your Reddit account, Reddit content, or Reddit's platform should be directed to Reddit. Requests concerning the CavsBot API may also involve Microsoft Azure or the API operator.

## Children's privacy

CavsBot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information to CavsBot, contact us at **caleb.keith@outlook.com**.

## Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the app to protect information handled by CavsBot. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
